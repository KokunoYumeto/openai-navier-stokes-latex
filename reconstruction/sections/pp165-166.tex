\NSi{NS-F-P165-001}{U=M=0} by Proposition~\ref{prop:A.4} and
\eqref{eq:C.17}; thus \NSi{NS-F-P165-002}{\mathcal A_X(U)=M/X=0}, and
\eqref{eq:4.7} gives \NSi{NS-F-P165-003}{V_0=0}. This proves the remaining
assertion of part~\textup{(v)}.

Finally, the third and fourth reserved intervals listed after \eqref{eq:A.9}
are the intervals \NSi{NS-F-P165-004}{\mathcal I_{\mathrm{pos}},
\mathcal I_{\mathrm{mean}}} in part~\textup{(vi)}. They lie in
\NSi{NS-F-P165-005}{(X_a,X_{\mathrm v})}, and their listed order gives
\NSi{NS-F-P165-006}{\sup\mathcal I_{\mathrm{pos}}<\inf\mathcal I_{\mathrm{mean}}}.
Proposition~\ref{prop:C.2} leaves them unchanged. The input profiles there
satisfy \NSi{NS-F-P165-007}{U=0} and
\NSi{NS-F-P165-008}{E=K(\eta)X^{-1/2-\lambda}}, with
\NSi{NS-F-P165-009}{K(\eta)=c_{\mathrm{patch}}(1+\eta^2)^{-1}} for a fixed
positive constant \NSi{NS-F-P165-010}{c_{\mathrm{patch}}}, by
Proposition~\ref{prop:A.4}. Thus they retain \eqref{eq:4.30}, completing
part~\textup{(vi)}. The finite frequency \NSi{NS-F-P165-011}{N} was fixed in
Proposition~\ref{prop:C.2} before the physical scale
\NSi{NS-F-P165-012}{q} and all later bands and correction stages, so the
constants have the independence asserted at the end of
Theorem~\ref{thm:4.6}.
\end{proof}
